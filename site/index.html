Everything from rig to render

One editor for game characters and animated film, with the tools that usually take three apps. Every card links to its guide in the documentation.

AutoRig

Import a layered PSD or SVG and get an editable rig: bones, joints, bending meshes and IK, in one step.

Retargeting

Animate once, reuse on every character. Bones are matched by name and motion scales to fit.

Full rigging toolset

Weighted meshes, skins, events, clipping and paths, with IK, physics and slider constraints.

Animate with confidence

Dopesheet, graph editor with Bézier handles, onion skin, a pose library and face controls.

Film workspace

An animated camera and audio tracks, rendered to PNG, WebM with alpha or MP4 at 4K.

Lip-sync from audio

Drop in a voice line and the mouth is keyed for you. Transcription runs on your computer.

Scenes

Several characters on one stage, with clips, voices, parallax layers and a moving camera.

Python and add-ons

Made for your setup

    Built for AI assistants

    Your assistant works on the project, not just about it

    Rigby2D ships a Model Context Protocol server. Any MCP-capable AI assistant can read rigs, evaluate poses with the real runtime, find export problems, add bones and eased keys, AutoRig a PSD, retarget animations, lip-sync dialogue and run Python, all inside the folders you allow.

    • Edits save in the file's own format
    • Many edits apply as one atomic batch
    • One-file install, or one command from source
