    Questions

    Good to know.

    Will Rigby2D stay free?

    Yes. The editor, the runtimes and the file formats are open source (MIT or Apache-2.0) and free to use in commercial games and films, with no runtime fees. Support never unlocks features.

    Where exactly does the money go?

    Into time on Rigby2D. Today it's built around a day job; each goal above replaces part of that job with Rigby2D work, first one day a week, then half time, then full time. After that, a small team. The devlog reports what came in each month and what it paid for.

    Why monthly?

    Time can only be planned on money that comes back. A monthly sponsorship, even $3, is what lets a day job turn into Rigby2D days. One-time support is just as welcome, and goes straight to the current goal.

    I don't have a GitHub account.

    Use Ko-fi: a card or PayPal, monthly or one-time, with the same tiers, and no account needed. Ko-fi supporters join the wall too.

    Can my studio sponsor?

    Yes. GitHub Sponsors works for organizations too, and the studio tiers, from $50 a month, put your logo here, in the README and on the home page (more for studios). For anything else, write to hello@rigby2d.com.

    Other ways to help

    Star the project on GitHub, share what you make on Discord, report bugs, write an add-on, or send a pull request. All of it counts.
