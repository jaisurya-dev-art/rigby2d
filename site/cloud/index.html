Rigby2D Cloud · coming soon

From one drawing to a rigged, animated character

Rigby2D rigs and animates for free. Cloud makes what a rig needs and you may not have: the separate parts, the missing art and the motion. It runs AI models too large for a laptop, as an optional subscription.

The waitlist opens soon. Until then, watch the repository on GitHub (Watch ▸ Custom ▸ Releases) to hear about the launch.

What Cloud does

Art to parts

Drop in one flat illustration and get it back in layers (head, torso, every limb, hair, eyes, mouth) with the areas hidden behind other parts painted in. The slowest step of 2D rigging, done in a minute.

The missing art, in your style

Mouth shapes for lip-sync, blinks, expressions, hands, a back or three-quarter view, new outfits as skins: drawn to match your character, so one drawing becomes a complete rig.

Motion from video

Film yourself on a phone and get keyframes on any rig, cleaned up and retargeted to cartoon proportions, ready to polish in the editor.

Share and review

Post a playable character to a link or embed it on your page, and send review links where your team comments on any frame in the browser.

Why a rig, not a sprite sheet

AI sprite generators give you fixed frames. A Rigby2D character is a skeleton: animations blend into each other, arms aim with IK, hair and cloth move with physics, outfits swap as skins, and it plays smoothly at any frame rate and resolution in a fraction of the file size. Cloud gets you there from a single drawing.

What stays free

Everything Rigby2D does today: the editor with its auto-rig and lip-sync, every runtime (JavaScript, Rust, Godot, Unity), the MCP server for AI assistants and the export formats. Rigs made with Cloud are ordinary Rigby2D files, so they keep working without it.
