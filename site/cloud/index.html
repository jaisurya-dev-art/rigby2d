Rigby2D Cloud · coming soon

From a drawing to a moving character in minutes

Rigby2D Cloud adds AI to the editor for the slow parts of rigging and animation. The editor and runtimes stay free and open source: Cloud is an optional subscription for the work that needs servers.

The waitlist opens soon. Until then, watch the repository on GitHub (Watch ▸ Custom ▸ Releases) to hear about the launch.

What Cloud does

Auto-rigging

Drop in a layered character and get a skeleton with bones placed on the joints, named and parented, ready to animate.

Meshes and weights

Bending meshes for every limb and smooth skin weights, generated from the art and the bones, then yours to refine.

Lip-sync from voice

Upload a voice line in any language and get timed visemes keyed on your character's mouth, synced to the audio.

Layer splitting

Turn a single flat image into separate parts (head, arms, legs, hair) with the hidden areas painted in, ready to rig.

What stays free

Everything Rigby2D does today: the editor, every runtime (JavaScript, Rust, Godot, Unity), the MCP server for AI assistants and the export formats. Rigs made with Cloud are ordinary Rigby2D files, so they keep working without it.
