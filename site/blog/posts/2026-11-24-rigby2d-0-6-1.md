Mammoth went out on Friday. Since then you've reported bugs, sent pull requests and asked for
things on Discord, and most of it is already in. 0.6.1 is that first round. If you have 0.6.0, the
editor offers the update by itself.

## Work on the part you're working on

Every bone and slot row in the Tree now has an **eye** and a **lock**. Hide the hair to work on the
face. Lock the body so a click on the head never grabs the torso instead. <kbd>Alt</kbd>+click does a
whole branch, and *View ▸ Show and unlock all* puts everything back. It only changes what you see
while you work: renders, exports and the runtimes always show everything.

## A Tree that reads like a hierarchy

- **Branch guides**: lines join each bone to its children, down every open branch, so deep rigs stay
  readable.
- **Collapse all** and **expand all** for the bones (thanks to @greeenos).
- **View ▸ Bone links**: dashed lines from bones to their parents in the viewport, for the selected
  branch, every bone, or none.

## Smaller things you asked for

- **Weights you can read**: in Weights mode, the vertex under the cursor shows how much each bone
  moves it, in percent.
- **Panels that stay on top**: in the desktop app, pin a popped-out panel and it stays above every
  other app, handy with a reference video or a second monitor.
- **Slot tint keys** in Animate mode, and every label now follows a change of language straight away.
- **A faster dopesheet** on long animations (thanks to @greeenos again).

## Physics that's already settled

Renders now let physics settle before the first frame, so hair, tails and cloth already hang at rest
instead of dropping into place on frame 1. Rigs without physics render exactly as before.

## For AI assistants: 60 tools

The MCP server grew from 45 to 60 tools. An assistant can now set **film effects** (shading, glow,
snow and rain), key **constraints**, make **meshes** from an image's outline, **bind and smooth
weights**, **bake physics** into ordinary keys, and **check animations** for jumps and loop seams.

## A tenth runtime, in preview

**SDL3** joins the runtimes: play Rigby2D animations in C++ games on SDL3's 2D renderer. Underneath
is the same C++ core as our Defold runtime, checked against the editor frame by frame. It's a preview
for now, so tell us how it runs in your game.

Also new: the PixiJS runtime is on npm as `@rigby2d/pixi-js` (thanks to @BlackRam-oss), and
[RigAxis](https://github.com/zrurf/RigAxis), zrurf's Unreal Engine 5 runtime, is listed in the docs
as our first community runtime.

## Fixes

Spine skeletons like Spineboy no longer spin their legs (thanks to @unreal79 for the report), the
Graph editor's curve preset menu opens again, and keyboard focus is visible in every dialog. The full
list is in the [release notes](https://github.com/jaisurya-dev-art/rigby2d/releases/tag/v0.6.1).
