Four days ago we passed 250 stars. Today the Rigby2D repository has **306**.

It's been five weeks since the repository went public on October 13. Every star, fork, bug report
and message on Discord has pushed Rigby2D further than we could have alone. Thank you.

## Five weeks in numbers

- ⭐ **306 stars** and **38 forks**
- 🚀 **18 releases**, from 0.1 to 0.5.1
- 🎮 **8 runtimes**, with a ninth arriving tomorrow
- 🌍 **Our first community runtime**: [RigAxis](/blog/rigaxis.html) brought Rigby2D to Unreal Engine 5
- 💬 **A growing [Discord](https://discord.gg/PGgaTwbNH)**, where much of the next release began

## Mammoth lands tomorrow

**Rigby2D 0.6 Mammoth comes out tomorrow, Friday 20 November**, our biggest release yet.

Its trailer premieres on YouTube the same evening, at **8:30 PM IST** (10:00 AM in New York,
3:00 PM in London). Everything in it was made in Rigby2D.

[**Set a reminder on YouTube**](https://youtu.be/Yqh1_XLBHsU)
