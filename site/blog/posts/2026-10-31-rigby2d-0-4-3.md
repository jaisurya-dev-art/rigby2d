Rigby2D 0.4.3 is for everyone with DragonBones characters, and everyone making games in Cocos
Creator. Your existing characters open in Rigby2D, exactly as they were, and play in Cocos.

[Download 0.4.3](https://github.com/jaisurya-dev-art/rigby2d/releases/tag/v0.4.3) or
[open the editor in your browser](https://rigby2d.com/editor/). If you have 0.4.2, the editor
offers the update by itself. [中文版本](/blog/rigby2d-0-4-3-zh.html).

## Cocos Creator runtime

![NILA and Rigbytron running in Cocos Creator 3.8](/docs/images/devlog-cocos.jpg)

**RigbySkeleton** brings Rigby2D characters to Cocos Creator 3.8:

- **A 2D renderer.** Under a Canvas it draws in order with your sprites and UI, follows the node's
  color and opacity, and works in 2D-only projects: no 3D engine module needed.
- **Everything plays.** Meshes and weights, IK and physics, clipping, two-color tint, every blend
  mode, skins, events and layered tracks with crossfades, from the same runtime as the editor.
- **Drop in your files.** Cocos turns the exported `.json` + `.atlas` into a Skeleton Data asset;
  drag it onto the component and pick an animation. `.skel` works too.
- **RigbyBoneFollower** keeps any node on a bone: a weapon, particles, a collider.

*File ▸ Get runtimes* now has a Cocos Creator card that installs the runtime straight into your
project. Tested in Cocos Creator 3.8.8. Guides in [English](https://rigby2d.com/docs/runtimes.html#cocos-creator)
and [中文](https://github.com/jaisurya-dev-art/rigby2d/blob/develop/runtimes/cocos/README.zh-CN.md).

## DragonBones projects, exactly as they were

The DragonBones importer was checked against the official DragonBones runtime on DragonBones' own
demo characters: across 55 animations, every bone, image and color matches within 0.1 px.

- **Nested armatures**: weapons, muzzle flashes and slash effects placed inside a slot now come in
  complete, playing exactly when the character swings or fires.
- **Fixes** found along the way: IK legs that bent the wrong way in some files, and swords that spun
  the long way round.

See them live, playing on the Rigby2D runtime, at [rigby2d.com](https://rigby2d.com/#dragonbones),
and read [how the comparison worked](https://rigby2d.com/docs/migrating-from-dragonbones.html).
