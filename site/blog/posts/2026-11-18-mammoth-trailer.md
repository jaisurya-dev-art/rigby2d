Rigby2D 0.6 is called **Mammoth**, and it's our biggest release yet. It gets a trailer to match.

**The trailer premieres on YouTube on Friday, 20 November, at 8:30 PM IST** (10:00 AM in New York,
3:00 PM in London), right after 0.6 is released.

[**Set a reminder on YouTube**](https://youtu.be/Yqh1_XLBHsU)

## Made in Rigby2D

Everything in the trailer was made in Rigby2D: the mammoth, its rig and animation, the snow and the
light. The editor footage is the real editor, recorded as it runs.

We won't spoil the rest here. Five chapters, one mammoth, and a lot of new things to try on Friday.

## Watch it with us

Join the premiere's live chat, and come to our [Discord](https://discord.gg/PGgaTwbNH) after to tell
us what you think. When it ends, 0.6 will be out: download it from GitHub, or open it in your
browser at [rigby2d.com](https://rigby2d.com).

See you on Friday. 🦣
