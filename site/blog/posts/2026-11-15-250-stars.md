Five days ago we passed 200 stars. Today the Rigby2D repository has **255**.

Five weeks ago, on October 13, the repository went public. Since then you've starred it, forked it,
filed bugs with perfect repro steps, and told us what you need on Discord. Thank you, every one of you.

## Five weeks in numbers

- ⭐ **255 stars** and **29 forks**
- 🚀 **18 releases**, from 0.1 to 0.5.1
- 🎮 **8 runtimes**: Godot, Unity, Defold, Cocos Creator, Phaser, PixiJS, Rust and JavaScript
- 🎬 **Our first Founding Creator**: [InkThorn Games](https://www.youtube.com/@InkThornGames) made a
  video about Rigby2D, now on the [homepage](/#creators)
- 💬 **A busy [Discord](https://discord.gg/PGgaTwbNH)**, where most of the next release started

## Something big on ice

0.5 was our biggest update. **0.6 is bigger.** We've been calling it **Mammoth**, and it comes out
this Friday.

We're keeping the details under wraps until then, but here's what we can say:

- **It answers you.** Much of it began as a message on Discord or an issue on GitHub. When you see
  it, you'll recognise your own requests.
- **It's for riggers.** Some of the things you've asked for most, and a few that Spine doesn't have.
- **You'll notice it the moment you open the editor.**

Follow [@Rigby2d on X](https://x.com/Rigby2d) for a glimpse each day until Friday.

## How you can help

- ⭐ **[Star Rigby2D on GitHub](https://github.com/jaisurya-dev-art/rigby2d)** if you haven't yet.
  It's the easiest way to help other animators find it.
- 💬 **[Join our Discord](https://discord.gg/PGgaTwbNH)** and be there when Mammoth lands.
- 🖌️ **Enter the [art challenge](/blog/art-challenge.html)**, open until December 7. Winners
