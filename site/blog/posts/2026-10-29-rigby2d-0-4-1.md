The DragonBones Pro desktop editor is no longer updated (its team moved on to LoongBones, a web
editor). Spine asks for a license before you ship. Rigby2D 0.4.1 is our answer: a free, open-source
editor that opens what you already made, and runtimes you can ship with no fees, ever.

[Download 0.4.1](https://github.com/jaisurya-dev-art/rigby2d/releases/tag/v0.4.1) or
[open the editor in your browser](https://rigby2d.com/editor/).

## Bring your characters

- **DragonBones**: *File ▸ Open* reads `_ske.json` projects with their `_tex.json` atlas. Bones,
  slots, images, weighted meshes, IK, skins and every animation come across, converted from
  DragonBones' Y-down space so they look and move the same.
  [Coming from DragonBones](https://rigby2d.com/docs/migrating-from-dragonbones.html).
- **Spine exports** (JSON and `.skel`, 3.8 to 4.3) open losslessly, and *File ▸ Import ▸ Check a
  skeleton file* tells you exactly what carries over before you switch.
  [Coming from Spine](https://rigby2d.com/docs/migrating-from-spine.html).

## A welcome screen

New projects, your recent work, starter projects (including a playable Phaser game with NILA), the
tour and the tutorials, the moment the editor opens.

![The welcome screen](/docs/images/devlog-welcome.jpg)

## Runtimes, one click away

*File ▸ Get runtimes* downloads the Unity package, the Godot addon for your system (or installs it
straight into your Godot project) and the Phaser bundles, always matching your editor's version.

![Get runtimes](/docs/images/devlog-runtimes.jpg)

## Add-ons, sandboxed

Python scripts and add-ons now run in a sandbox: they can change your project, but they can't
touch your files or the network. *Preferences ▸ Add-ons ▸ Get add-ons* opens the gallery, with
three official add-ons to start: Mirror tools, Breathing idle and Rig report.

![The add-on gallery](/docs/images/devlog-addons.jpg)

## And

- **Auto-update**: the desktop app checks for new versions and installs signed updates in place.
- **Six languages**: English, 日本語, 简体中文, 한국어, Español and Português (Brasil).
- The README, in all six languages too.
