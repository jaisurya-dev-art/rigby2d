Four weeks ago, on October 13, the Rigby2D repository went public. Today it has **216 stars**.

For an open-source animation tool built by a tiny team, that's a lot of people telling us we're onto
something. Thank you, every one of you.

## Four weeks in numbers

- ⭐ **216 stars** and **25 forks**
- 🚀 **17 releases**, from 0.1 to 0.5
- 🎮 **8 runtimes**: Godot, Unity, Defold, Cocos Creator, Phaser, Rust, JavaScript and PixiJS
  (PixiJS coming in 0.5.1)
- 🌏 **A full Simplified Chinese interface**, coming in 0.5.1
- 💬 **A new home on [Discord](https://discord.gg/PGgaTwbNH)**, and our first community art challenge

## You shaped it

Most of what shipped this month started as a message from one of you: the PixiJS runtime, the pixel
grid, opening files from the command line, the Chinese interface, and a pile of bug reports with
perfect repro steps. Keep them coming. Issues on GitHub and **#feature-requests** on Discord are
where the roadmap really gets written.

## What's next

- **0.5.1** in a few days: the Chinese interface, the PixiJS runtime, the pixel grid, Setup and
  Animate tabs, boomerang playback, and fixes you reported.
- **0.6**: warp deformers, and a cleaner layout for the editor.
- **The art challenge** is open until December 7. Winners ship inside Rigby2D.

## How you can help

- ⭐ **[Star Rigby2D on GitHub](https://github.com/jaisurya-dev-art/rigby2d)** if you haven't yet.
  It's the easiest way to help other animators find it.
- 💬 **[Join our Discord](https://discord.gg/PGgaTwbNH)** and show us what you're making.
- 🖌️ **Enter the art challenge** and put your character in front of every Rigby2D user.

On to 500. 🚀
