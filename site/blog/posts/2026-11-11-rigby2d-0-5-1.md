Rigby2D 0.5.1: a **PixiJS runtime**, **Rigby2D models that play live in the Defold editor**, the editor **fully in Simplified Chinese**, and a long list of animator requests from our community. If you have 0.5.0, the editor offers this update by itself.

[Download 0.5.1](https://github.com/jaisurya-dev-art/rigby2d/releases/tag/v0.5.1) or [open the editor in your browser](https://rigby2d.com/editor/).

> [!NOTE]
> **Linux files are coming shortly.** GitHub's Linux build machines can't reach Ubuntu's package servers right now, so the AppImage, the `.deb` and the Linux Godot runtime will be added to this release as soon as they build. Everything else is here.

## What's new

### PixiJS runtime

Rigby2D characters now play natively in **PixiJS 8**, on WebGL and WebGPU ([guide](https://github.com/jaisurya-dev-art/rigby2d/blob/main/runtimes/pixi/README.md)).

- A skeleton is an ordinary PixiJS `Container`: filters, masks, tint, blend modes, bounds and pointer events all work, and it batches with your sprites.
- Everything plays, exactly as in the editor: meshes and weights, IK, transform, path, physics and slider constraints, clipping, skins, two-color tint, events, and layered tracks with crossfades and queues.
- **Optional WebAssembly core**: the Rust runtime compiled to WASM (about 150 KB gzipped), about twice as fast as the JavaScript path for crowds.

### Defold: Rigby2D models, live in the editor

A new **Rigby2D Model** component (`.rigbymodel`), like a sprite or a Spine model: add it to a game object and the Defold editor plays it in the scene view. Pick the animation and skin in the Properties panel and press Space to watch it move ([guide](https://github.com/jaisurya-dev-art/rigby2d/blob/main/runtimes/defold/README.md#rigby2d-models-live-in-the-editor)).

- **One-click export**: *File ▸ Export ▸ Defold model…* writes the skeleton, the images with a Defold atlas, the component and a ready game object into your Defold project.
- **Drawn natively** in the game, driven by `rigby.play`, `rigby.queue`, `rigby.on_event`, `rigby.bone` and `go.get` / `go.set`.
- Uses a regular Defold atlas, so texture profiles and compression apply. The editor plugin runs on Windows, macOS and Linux.
- Also from the AI assistant: the `export_defold_model` MCP tool.

### Simplified Chinese

The whole editor interface is now in Simplified Chinese (*Preferences ▸ General ▸ Language*).

### Animating

- **Setup and Animate tabs** in the menu bar; the timeline hides in Setup to give the viewport room.
- **Timeline bar**: frame rate, playback speed and timecode where you look, and **boomerang** playback (to the end and back).
- **Zoom the timeline**: <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+wheel zooms time around the cursor, <kbd>Alt</kbd>+wheel pans.
- **Cut, copy and paste keys** from the right-click menu, with *Paste here* at the frame you clicked.
- **Curve of new keys**: start new keys linear, eased or stepped (*Preferences ▸ Editing*).

### Rigging and the viewport

- **Bone colors**: give bones their own color to tell limbs and sides apart.
- **Hold Shift while translating** to move along one axis.
- **Show or hide each overlay**: bones, clipping masks, bounding boxes, paths, points, or all at once.
- **Pixel grid** for pixel art, with the grid on pixel edges.
- **Undo, redo, fit and show-bones buttons** next to the mode tabs, and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>B</kbd> to show or hide bones.
- **Slots fold** in the tree, showing only the visible image, so busy rigs stay readable.
- **AutoRig imports PNGs** straight from its dialog.

### Also new

- **Open files from your desktop**: double-click a `.rigby` file, or drop files on the window (#35).
- **Tooltips show shortcuts** in the keys of your keymap.
- **Interface scale** is a slider from 75% to 200%.
- The editor window opens maximized; the renderer and shortcut presets live in *Preferences*.
- **Help menu** with a Support dialog, and our community is now on [Discord](https://discord.gg/PGgaTwbNH).

### Fixes

- **Stretching two-bone IK** reaches its target when the chain is bent (every runtime).
- **Weight painting keeps a mesh's deform keys** (#36).
- **Image sequences** read their base name, start and digits from the image (`guy-01` gives `guy-01`, `guy-02`…).
- **Child bones** added from a bone keep its length instead of shrinking.
- The desktop app no longer shows the browser's right-click menu (#34).

Everything from 0.5.0 is included: the Defold runtime, sprite sheet export, SVG import, mesh from image and the production-ready Godot runtime.
