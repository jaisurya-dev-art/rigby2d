Rigby2D 0.6 Mammoth is our biggest release yet: **a new interface built on one design language**, **dockable panels with tabs**, **film effects**, **2.5D turns and warp deformers**, rigging tools that keep up with you, **Aseprite import**, a **command-line tool** and a **Ren'Py runtime**. If you have 0.5, the editor offers this update by itself.

[Download 0.6.0](https://github.com/jaisurya-dev-art/rigby2d/releases/tag/v0.6.0) or [open the editor in your browser](https://rigby2d.com/editor/).

## What's new

### A new interface

Every panel, dialog, toolbar and label was rebuilt on one design system.

- **One look everywhere**: shared colours, spacing and type, and one set of buttons, toggles, fields and dialogs. Escape and clicking outside close every dialog the same way.
- **Real icons**: one SVG icon set (Lucide) across the toolbars, timeline, tree and inspector. No emoji.
- **Tree and inspector rebuilt**, with shorter, consistent wording and proper plurals.
- **Themes**: Rigby Dark, **Mammoth** and Rigby Light, or make your own and share it as a `.rigbytheme` file (*View ▸ Themes…*). The colour picker and every control follow the theme.
- **Checked automatically**: every main view and 15 dialogs at three window sizes, in English and Chinese, with keyboard focus that stays inside dialogs.

### Your layout

- **Dock any panel**: drag it by its tab to the left, right or bottom, or into another panel's tabs.
- **Saved layouts**, in the browser editor too: *View ▸ Layouts* keeps where panels sit and how wide they are (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>1</kbd>–<kbd>9</kbd>).
- **Rig panel**: skins, constraints by type, meshes and events in one place, so the tree keeps just bones, draw order, animations and images.
- A tighter first layout that gives the viewport more room.

### Film effects

For short films and cutscenes, in the Film workspace:

- **Layer effects** on any bone or slot: drop shadow, light shading with a rim light, glow, blur and colour grade.
- **Particles**: snow, dust, sparks and rain from emitters on bones, deterministic so every render matches the preview.
- **Motion blur** and **depth of field** in renders, and a colour grade for the whole shot.
- Live in the viewport and in every render path, on the GPU. Game exports never see them.

### 2.5D turn and warp deformers

- **Make turnable**: select a head's parts and one control bone turns and tilts it in depth. It plays in every runtime, and games can drive it live.
- **Shape a side by hand**: fix any turn angle with a lattice, and the in-between angles blend.
- **Warp deformers**: nested lattices over meshes, keyable, and resizable without losing their keys. Baked to mesh deform keys, so every runtime plays them.
- Additive slider constraints now work in every runtime, so Spine files that use them play correctly.

### Rigging that keeps up with you

- **Test pose while painting weights**: <kbd>Alt</kbd>+drag any bone and keep painting on the posed mesh. **Wiggle** swings the bone while you paint.
- **Weights button** on the toolbar: one click from a mesh to painting its weights.
- **Bone only**: in Setup mode, move, turn or scale a bone and its children and images stay where they are drawn, IK chains included.
- **Transform constraints that never jump**, a clear Driver and Driven, offsets set by moving the bone, and pick buttons to choose bones by clicking.
- **Point bones** (#37): bones with no length, for pivots and anchors.
- **Better mesh generation** (#38): locked vertices, every shape in an image, holes, and a minimum angle.
- **Find** in the tree and **drag to reparent**.
- **Tween lines** in the dopesheet show where values blend between keys.

### Pipeline

- **Aseprite import**: `.aseprite` and `.ase` files open directly. Layers become parts, tags become animations at Aseprite's timing, hidden layers become variant animations, and blend modes are kept.
- **`rigby` on the command line**: inspect, validate, convert, export, AutoRig, run Python scripts, call any MCP tool, and render on the GPU, for scripts and CI ([docs](https://rigby2d.com/docs/cli.html)).
- **Ren'Py runtime**, the ninth: characters with expressions, lip-sync from voice lines, blinks, gaze and 2.5D turns in visual novels. Pure Python, golden-tested against the core. Runtime and demo zips are attached below.
- **MCP**: add, edit and delete constraints without the editor (45 tools).

### Also new

- **The Mammoth demo** (*File ▸ Open demo*): the star of 0.6, with its walk, its trumpet call and film effects.
- **A new splash screen** with a Start button. Turn its animation off in *Preferences ▸ General*.
- **A new What's new tour** for 0.6.

### Fixes

- Spine 4.3 `.skel` files with local, additive or clamped transform constraints open, and exports keep those options.
- Shortcuts work on Russian and other non-Latin keyboard layouts.
- The tree lists a bone's parts front first, like the draw order.
- Pixel art turns the pixel grid on by itself on import.
- Layer sets picked on the desktop keep their names and order.
