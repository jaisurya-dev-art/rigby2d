For years, 2D skeletal animation for games has come with a price on the way in. Meshes and IK need
Spine Professional at $379, and you need a license before you ship the game you made. Rigby2D's
answer has always been the same: every feature free, every runtime free to ship, open source. 0.5 is
the release where that answer covers more than ever.

[Download 0.5](https://github.com/jaisurya-dev-art/rigby2d/releases/tag/v0.5.0),
let the editor update itself, or [open it in your browser](https://rigby2d.com/editor/).

## What's in 0.5

- **A native Defold runtime.** C++, on every platform Defold ships to: Windows, macOS, Linux,
  Android, iOS and HTML5, matching the editor frame by frame. Add it as a library and it plays
  meshes, IK, physics, skins and scenes. [How it was built](/blog/defold-and-sprite-sheets.html).
- **Sprite sheet export, on the GPU.** A packed sheet with JSON for Phaser and PixiJS, a Defold
  atlas, or PNG frames. Sheets too big for one image split into several, and the export tells you
  the size before it starts.
- **SVG import.** Inkscape and Affinity drawings come in layer by layer, each part where you drew
  it, ready for AutoRig. It started as [a post on r/rigby2d](/blog/two-posts-two-features.html).
- **Generate a mesh from an image.** One click traces the art, places the points and builds the
  triangles. Then bend it.
- **Godot, production-ready.** RigbySkeleton2D and RigbyScene2D are out of preview.
- **And more:** your own keyboard shortcuts, copy and paste of keys in the timeline, layered PNGs
  that land in place, opening projects in the browser editor by picking or dropping files, a dialog
  after every export, and Phaser 4 support.

Seven runtimes now play what you make in Rigby2D, and every one is checked against the same
reference: [Seven runtimes, one answer](/blog/seven-runtimes-one-answer.html).

## Bring your Spine and DragonBones projects

Rigby2D opens Spine exports (JSON and `.skel`, 3.8 to 4.3) without loss, and DragonBones projects
with their atlas. *File ▸ Import ▸ Check a skeleton file* tells you what carries over before you
switch. The guides: [coming from Spine](https://rigby2d.com/docs/migrating-from-spine.html) and
[coming from DragonBones](https://rigby2d.com/docs/migrating-from-dragonbones.html).

## Get it

- **Desktop:** [Windows, macOS and Linux](https://github.com/jaisurya-dev-art/rigby2d/releases/tag/v0.5.0).
  If you have 0.4, the editor offers this update by itself.
- **Browser:** [rigby2d.com/editor](https://rigby2d.com/editor/), nothing to install.
- **Runtimes:** Unity, Godot, Defold, Cocos Creator, Phaser, JavaScript and Rust, on the
  [runtimes page](https://rigby2d.com/runtimes/) or from *File ▸ Get runtimes* in the editor.
