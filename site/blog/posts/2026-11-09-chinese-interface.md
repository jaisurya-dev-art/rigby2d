When we shipped 0.5, a Chinese animator on YouTube asked us a fair question: *why is only the menu
bar in Chinese?* They were right. Our translations stopped at the top of the window. Every panel,
dialog and status message below it was still in English.

We promised them a full Chinese interface in the next release. It's done.

## 简体中文, all of it

In **Rigby2D 0.5.1**, set *Preferences ▸ General ▸ Language* to **简体中文**, or just let
Rigby2D follow your system language. You'll get Chinese text everywhere:

- **Every panel**: the tree, the inspector, the timeline, the dopesheet and the graph editor.
- **Every tool and dialog**: constraints, meshes and weights, AutoRig, retargeting, poses and face
  controls, lip-sync, scenes, rendering and sprite sheet export.
- **Every message**: status lines, import warnings (DragonBones files included) and errors.
- **The tutorials**: all four lessons, step by step, from the first click to your first animation.

That's about 1,450 translated texts, up from 400. A test now fails the build if any interface text
is missing its Chinese translation, so new features will arrive in Chinese too.

Japanese, Korean, Spanish and Portuguese still cover only part of the interface. They're next, and if
you speak one of them and would like to help, tell us on Discord.

0.5.1 also brings a few smaller things: AutoRig can import PNGs straight from its own window, and the
Help menu has a new home for support and community links. The release is coming soon.

## Our home is Discord now

After Reddit banned r/rigby2d ([here's what happened](https://rigby2d.com/blog/2026-11-07-fck-reddit.html)),
we moved the community to Discord, and it has already grown into a nice place to hang out.

**[Join the Rigby2D Discord](https://discord.gg/PGgaTwbNH)**

Show your work in **#showcase**, ask in **#help**, shape the next version in **#feature-requests**,
and our Chinese-speaking users have their own **#中文** channel. *File ▸ Share to Discord…* in the
editor turns your animation into a GIF, ready to post.

Something for artists is starting there soon. Keep an eye on the server.
