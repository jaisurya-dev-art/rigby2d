From today, every Rigby2D release and every bit of news lands here first, on the devlog, and in the
editor itself: the welcome screen now shows what's new.

## Follow along

- **In the editor**: the newest posts appear on the welcome screen.
- **RSS**: [rigby2d.com/blog/feed.xml](https://rigby2d.com/blog/feed.xml) in any feed reader.
- **r/rigby2d**: our own community on Reddit, for showing what you made, asking for help and
  talking about what's next. [Join r/rigby2d](https://www.reddit.com/r/rigby2d/).
- **GitHub**: star or watch [the repository](https://github.com/jaisurya-dev-art/rigby2d) for
  releases.

## Show us what you make

Rigs, animations, games and films made with Rigby2D are what this community is for. Post them on
r/rigby2d with the *Showcase* flair. The best ones may end up in a future devlog post, or in the
editor's starter projects.
