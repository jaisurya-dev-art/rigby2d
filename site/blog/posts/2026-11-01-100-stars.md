Rigby2D passed **100 stars on GitHub** today, nineteen days after its first commit.

![Rigbytron holding up a gold star next to "100 stars on GitHub"](/docs/images/devlog-100-stars.jpg)

For a free 2D animation tool that started as one person's project, that is a lot of people deciding
it was worth keeping an eye on. Thank you, every one of you.

## Nineteen days

Here is what those days held:

- **16 releases**, from 0.1 on 15 October to [0.4.3](/blog/rigby2d-0-4-3.html) yesterday, and
  231 commits.
- **Runtimes for six engines**: Godot, Unity, Cocos Creator, Phaser, the web and Rust, all playing
  the same animation frame for frame.
- **DragonBones projects that open as they were**, weapons and effects included, checked against
  the DragonBones runtime within 0.1 px.
- **Ten pull requests from a contributor**, maruccimauro, who brought detachable panels, onion
  skinning, guides and copy and paste of keys. [The full story](/blog/built-in-the-open.html).
- **An [add-on marketplace](/addons/)**, [a devlog](/blog/), and a community on
  [r/rigby2d](https://www.reddit.com/r/rigby2d/).
- **People talking about it**: Alex Hitt's
  [technical review](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K9hDnlXZ2No), a feature in
  [Polydium's](https://polydium.net/briefing/8df9db93-0abf-4032-bd88-acbdf95ec431) briefing for
  Korean studios, and a place on [Trendshift's](https://trendshift.io/topics/game-engine) game engine
  list.
- **1,600+ downloads** from the release page, on top of everyone using the
  [browser editor](https://rigby2d.com/editor/).

## Why the stars matter

A star costs nothing, but for an open-source project it is how the next person decides whether to
trust it. Rigby2D asks animators to move their characters, their files and their games' runtimes to
something new. Every star says *someone already did, and it held up*. That is what brings the next
animator, the next bug report and the next contributor.

## What's next

This week is about listening: the bugs you hit, the features you miss, the DragonBones and Spine
files that don't import the way they should. Those decide what goes into the next release. The
[issues on GitHub](https://github.com/jaisurya-dev-art/rigby2d/issues) and the threads on
[r/rigby2d](https://www.reddit.com/r/rigby2d/) are where that happens, and every one gets read.

Here's to the next hundred.
