Here's the honest truth: **Rigby2D can do far more than our demos show.** We're developers, not
artists. NILA and Rigbytron are simple rigs, and they don't come close to what meshes, IK, physics,
skins and face poses can do in the hands of a real artist.

So we're handing the stage to you. Make the characters that show what Rigby2D can really do.

**[Join the challenge on Discord](https://discord.gg/PGgaTwbNH)**

## Two tracks

Enter one or both. When you post, pick the matching tag:

- 🖌️ **Art only**: a layered character made for rigging. A PSD or SVG with one layer per body part,
  and the layers named (head, torso, arm_l…), so AutoRig can read them.
- 🦴 **Art + Rig**: a rigged, animated character as a `.rigby` file. Show off meshes, IK, physics,
  skins, face poses or lip-sync.

No install needed: the [browser editor](https://rigby2d.com/editor/) has everything.

## You pick the winners

The posts with the **most reactions and comments** in **#art-challenge** win, one per track. So bring
your friends, share your post everywhere, and get people talking. Every reaction and every comment
counts.

- 📅 **Open now**, closes **Sunday, December 7** (end of day, UTC)
- 🥇 **Winners announced Sunday, December 14**

## How to submit

1. Join the [Rigby2D Discord](https://discord.gg/PGgaTwbNH) and open **#art-challenge**.
2. Click **New Post** and use your character's name as the title.
3. Pick a tag: **Art only** or **Art + Rig**.
4. Attach a preview (an image or a GIF) and your source file (PSD, SVG or `.rigby`). In the editor,
   *File ▸ Share to Discord…* turns your animation into a GIF for you.
5. Add this line to your post: `License: CC BY 4.0, by <your name>`

## What winners get

- ⭐ **Your character ships inside Rigby2D** as an official demo in *File ▸ Open demo*, credited to
  you and seen by everyone who opens the editor.
- ⭐ **The splash screen**: your art greets every user when Rigby2D starts.
- ⭐ **The rigby2d.com homepage**, plus a gallery page that links to your portfolio.
- ⭐ **An Artist Spotlight devlog**: an interview about you and your work, in English and Chinese.
- ⭐ **A starring role in the next release trailer.**
- ⭐ **Art only winner: we rig and animate your character** and ship it, and you get the full rigged
  file.
- ⭐ **Credited forever** in *Help ▸ About*, plus the **Featured Artist** role on Discord.
- ⭐ **Early access** to new features before they're released.

## The rules

- **Original work only. No AI-generated art.**
- Entries are shared under **[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)**: you keep your
  copyright, anyone can learn from and reuse your character, and every use credits you. That's how
  community demos stay free for everyone to study.
- One post per character. You can enter both tracks.
- Fake accounts or bought reactions disqualify an entry, and spam replies don't count.

## New to rigging?

Start with *Help ▸ Tutorials* in the editor (now in 简体中文 too): in about fifteen minutes it takes
you from a layered drawing to a moving character. Ask anything in **#help** on Discord. We're there
every day.

We can't wait to see what you make. 💙
