Learn from finished rigs

Three projects made in Rigby2D, from a single robot to a whole scene. Open one in the browser editor to see how it is built, or download it with its game-ready export to try the runtimes.

NILA

A layered character with bending meshes, physics on her braid and jhumkas, face poses with a Mood control, her voice with viseme lip-sync, and the 70-second promo film.

  • Meshes
  • Physics
  • Face poses
  • Lip-sync
  • Film workspace

Scene: NILA meets Rigbytron

Scene composition: both characters as actors with clips, her line with its voice and lip-sync, then his hello in a robot voice, on a painted multiplane stage with parallax and a moving camera.
